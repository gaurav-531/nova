FAQ

Sawaal

Play Store par kyun nahi hai?

Nova poora phone chalane ke liye Accessibility, SMS aur call log jaisi permissions use karta hai. Play Store aisi automation apps ko bahut kam allow karta hai, isliye Nova seedha is website se milta hai.

Play Protect ne "App blocked" kyun dikhaya?

India mein Google un apps ko rokta hai jo website ya WhatsApp se install hon aur SMS, notification ya Accessibility (screen control) ki permission maangein. Nova ko ye permissions apne kaam ke liye chahiye, isliye block dikhta hai. Install karne ke liye: Play Store › profile photo › Play Protect › settings › "Scan apps with Play Protect" thodi der OFF, APK install karo, phir wapas ON kar do. Ye sirf isi website wale APK ke liye karna. Call chalte waqt ye setting lock rehti hai.

Screen control (Accessibility) safe hai?

Nova sirf aapke bolne par tap ya scroll karta hai, screen record nahi karta, aur GPay / PhonePe jaise payment apps mein khud band ho jata hai. Advanced "auto mode" mein ye sirf "Nova" bolne par ON hota hai aur 2 minute baad khud band.

Kya meri awaaz ya data kahin jaata hai?

Jagane wala shabd aur Voice ID phone ke andar chalte hain. Command sunne ke liye Google ki speech service use hoti hai (band kar sakte ho). Gemini sirf tab, jab aap apni key daalo. Poori list Privacy Policy mein.

Gemini key zaroori hai?

Nahi. Calls, messages, screen control, yaadein, college, automation, SOS aur hisaab bina key ke chalte hain. Key daalne par mushkil sawaal, likhna, translate, camera se dekhna, lecture notes aur HD awaaz bhi milte hain.

Phone badal liya to?

Purane phone mein Voice tab › "License / Kharido" › "Is phone se hatao" dabao, phir naye phone mein wahi key daalo. Purana phone kho gaya ho to support ko email karo.

Payment ho gaya par key nahi aayi?

Spam folder dekho. 1 ghante mein na mile to order ID ke saath support ko email karo.

Refund milega?

Pehle free try karo. Kharidne ke 7 din ke andar Nova aapke phone par theek se na chale aur support theek na kar paaye, to poora refund. Detail Refund policy mein.
