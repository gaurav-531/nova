Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Short version: Nova runs on your phone. The developer has no server and does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Some features send data to a third party only when you use them; every one is listed below.

Who we are

Nova Voice Assistant ("Nova", "we") is developed by Gourav Sanwariya, Udaipur, Rajasthan, India. Contact: support@yournovasite.com.

Data that stays on your phone

DataWhy Nova uses it
MicrophoneTo hear the wake word "Nova" and your command. The wake phrase is detected on the device. Audio is not stored, except when you say "lecture record karo": that recording is deleted as soon as the notes are made.
Voice IDIf you register your voice, Nova keeps a numeric voice profile on the phone so it wakes only for you. You can delete it from the Voice tab.
Contacts, call log, SMSCalling and messaging by name, missed calls, spending summaries from bank SMS, SOS messages and the "find my phone" SMS code.
NotificationsReading messages aloud and replying, alerts you set ("tell me when ... arrives"), and new-email announcements.
Accessibility (screen control)Tapping, scrolling, typing and switching settings when you ask. Nova does not record the screen. Screen control switches itself off when a payment or banking app opens. In the optional auto mode it is turned on only when you say "Nova" and off again about 2 minutes later.
Camera, photos, filesTaking photos, making PDFs and collages, and finding files or photos you ask for.
LocationNearest hospital, weather and rain alerts, parking spot, sending your location, SOS.
Calendar, steps, usage accessBriefings and plans, step count, screen time and app limits.
Memories and organiserMemories, tasks, goals, inbox, people notes, conversation history and music taste. OTPs, passwords and card numbers are removed before anything is saved. Delete from the Brain tab, or export with "meri yaadein export karo".

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not run a server that stores your data.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may access, correct and erase your data and withdraw consent. Because the data is on your phone, you can do this directly: clear memories in the Brain tab, export them, remove Voice ID from the Voice tab, withdraw consent from the Help tab, or uninstall the app. For anything else, email us.

Children

Nova is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We update this page and the in-app policy together. After a material change the app asks for your consent again.
