Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Short version: Use the free trial before you buy. If Nova does not work on your phone after purchase, ask within 7 days for a full refund.

Free trial first

Every install includes a free trial with all features (30 days during the beta, 3 days after), so you can check that Nova works on your phone before paying.

When you can get a refund

  • You ask within 7 days of purchase, and
  • Nova does not work properly on your phone (for example, it cannot hear you or the main features fail) and support could not fix it, or
  • You were charged twice or charged by mistake.

When refunds are not given

  • More than 7 days after purchase.
  • The license key was shared or used against the Terms.
  • A feature that needs your own Gemini key does not work because the key is missing or over Google's limits.

How to ask

Email support@yournovasite.com with your order ID (from the Dodo Payments receipt), your phone model and a short description of the problem. We reply within 3 working days.

After a refund

The money goes back to your original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank. The license key is disabled after the refund.
